# ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Image Format Requirements

## ✅ Supported Formats
- **JPG/JPEG** ✅ (Recommended)
- **PNG** ✅
- **WEBP** ✅
- **GIF** ✅

## ❌ NOT Supported
- **HEIC/HEIF** ❌ (iPhone default format)

## Why HEIC Doesn't Work

HEIC is Apple's proprietary format. Web browsers cannot display HEIC files directly, and Google Drive's CORS policy prevents downloading them for conversion in the browser.

## Solution: Convert HEIC to JPG Before Uploading

### Option 1: On iPhone/Mac
1. Open the photo in **Photos** app
2. Tap **Share** → **Save to Files** or **AirDrop**
3. iOS will automatically convert to JPG when sharing
4. Upload the JPG file to Google Drive

### Option 2: Using Mac Preview
1. Open HEIC file in **Preview**
2. **File** → **Export**
3. Choose format: **JPEG**
4. Click **Save**

### Option 3: Bulk Convert Online
- Visit [heictojpg.com](https://heictojpg.com)
- Upload all HEIC files
- Download converted JPG files
- Upload to Google Drive

### Option 4: iPhone Settings (For Future Photos)
Change iPhone to save photos as JPG by default:
1. **Settings** → **Camera** → **Formats**
2. Select **Most Compatible** (instead of High Efficiency)
3. New photos will be saved as JPG

## Verifying Image Formats

Before uploading to Google Drive, check file extensions:
- ✅ `photo.jpg` or `photo.jpeg`
- ✅ `photo.png`
- ✅ `photo.webp`
- ❌ `photo.heic` or `photo.heif` - Convert first!
